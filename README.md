# Free invoice template (Excel / Google Sheets), English + Polish
# Darmowy szablon faktury (Excel / Arkusze Google), po polsku i po angielsku

A free one-page invoice: line items, VAT by rate, due date and amount due. Type in the yellow cells; everything else is calculated. Prints on one page.

## Files

- `invoice-en-blank.xlsx` (English)
- `invoice-pl-blank.xlsx` (Polski)

## Good to know

- The VAT rates are plain cells you can edit, so it works with any country's tax rates.
- Formula cells are locked without a password (Review > Unprotect Sheet to change one).
- Tested in LibreOffice. The author has not opened the files in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
- A bookkeeping aid, not tax, legal or financial advice.

**Polski:** wpisujesz w żółtych komórkach, resztę liczy arkusz. Stawki VAT to zwykłe komórki, które można zmienić. Testowano w LibreOffice; autor nie otwierał plików w Excelu ani w Arkuszach Google.
